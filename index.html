Rockefeller Center as a System

Rockefeller Center is built in layers. Visitors gather on the plaza to look up. Office workers move underneath it, through passages and platforms most visitors never see. This model follows both groups through a working day.

Click the model to interact. Drag to look around if the camera allows it.

Start here

  1. Pick a level with the Street, Concourse, and Subway buttons on the left of the model.
  2. Press Auto Play to run the clock through the day, or drag the slider to jump to a specific time.
  3. Watch the count under the clock. It shows how many workers and tourists are moving at that moment.

What to look for

  • Who stops and who passes throughTourists collect around the rink, the flags, and Atlas. Workers rarely slow down.
  • How the mix shifts over the dayCompare the morning rush with mid-afternoon, when the counts flip.
  • Who never comes upFollow a worker from the subway. Many reach their building without touching the street.
